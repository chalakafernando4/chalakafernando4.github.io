ATLAS Collaboration · CERN Summer Student Programme

Study of Higgs Boson Mass Reconstruction in tHq, H → ττ Final States Using Machine Learning

2024 · Completed

Particle PhysicsMachine LearningATLAS

Overview

As a CERN Summer Student working with the ATLAS Collaboration, I studied how machine learning can improve the reconstruction of the Higgs boson candidate mass in tHq production (Higgs boson produced in association with a single top quark), with the Higgs decaying to a pair of tau leptons (H → ττ). The analysis focused on final states with two light leptons and one hadronically decaying tau.

Motivation

The tHq process is a rare but valuable channel: it is directly sensitive to the coupling between the Higgs boson and the top quark, including its sign. A major challenge in the two-light-leptons-plus-hadronic-tau final state is the tZq background, which produces a very similar event signature. Because tau decays involve neutrinos that escape detection, the Higgs candidate mass cannot be measured directly — it must be reconstructed from incomplete information. A better mass estimate gives a better handle for separating tHq events (where the ττ pair comes from a Higgs boson) from tZq events (where the lepton pair comes from a Z boson).

Methods

  • Worked with simulated ATLAS analysis-level events for tHq signal and tZq background.
  • Preprocessed event-level data: selecting input variables (lepton and tau kinematics, missing transverse energy, and related quantities), cleaning, and normalising features.
  • Built and trained neural-network regression models in TensorFlow/Keras to estimate the mass of the di-tau system on an event-by-event basis.
  • Evaluated model performance against the true generated mass, and compared the reconstructed mass distributions for signal and background.
  • Used Python for the ML pipeline and ROOT/C++ for handling physics data formats.

Results & outputs

The trained regression models produced Higgs candidate mass estimates that can be used as a discriminating variable between tHq and tZq events.
