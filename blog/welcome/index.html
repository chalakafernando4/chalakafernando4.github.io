Welcome — why this site exists

20 May 2026

MetaIntroduction

Hello — I’m Chalaka, a physics graduate from Sri Lanka. I built this site so that the work I care about lives somewhere more permanent than a folder of PDFs: my research projects, the things I’m learning, and the road toward graduate study.

What you’ll find here

Research notes. Short write-ups of what I’m working on — machine learning for particle physics, astronomy data analysis, and experiments with new tools.

Physics explanations. Teaching undergraduate physics taught me that explaining something clearly is the best test of understanding it. I’ll occasionally write explainers on topics I enjoy — from Higgs physics to how astronomers actually measure double stars.

The scholarship road. Applying to international programmes from Sri Lanka involves a lot of figuring things out alone. As I go through MSc/PhD and scholarship applications, I’ll share what I learn — honestly, including the rejections.

Where I’m starting from

In brief: a BSc Honours in Physics at the University of Ruhuna, a summer at CERN with the ATLAS Collaboration working on machine-learning-based Higgs mass reconstruction, a final-year astronomy project on the visual binary STF 1434, and teaching experience as a temporary demonstrator and assistant lecturer. The full story is on the About page.

If anything here is useful to you — or if you spot a mistake — write to me. Thanks for reading.
