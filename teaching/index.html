Teaching & Outreach

Teaching

Explaining physics well is part of doing physics well. I have taught and demonstrated for undergraduate physics courses as a temporary academic staff member.

Current / Recent Role

Temporary Assistant Lecturer, Temporary Demonstrator in Physicsr

Department of Physics, University of Ruhuna, Sri Lanka · 2024 - Present

I support undergraduate physics education through lectures, laboratory demonstrations, tutorial classes, and course assistance — primarily in electricity and magnetism, thermodynamics and in computational physics. The role includes preparing teaching materials and multiple-choice assessments, and working closely with students at very different levels of preparation.

Volunteer Mentor

Volunteer Mentor for High School Astronomy students

Department of Physics, University of Ruhuna, Sri Lanka · 2025-Present

Through this role, I encourage highly talented secondary-school students to undertake their own research in double-star astronomy. It is a great privilege to inspire these young researchers and support their aspirations to become future scientists. As a volunteer mentor, I am committed to guiding them toward early publication—an uncommon achievement for school students in Sri Lanka, where astronomy remains significantly underrepresented.
