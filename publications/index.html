Peer-Reviewed Publications

  1. ASTROMETRIC MEASUREMENTS OF STF 1434 DOUBLE STAR SYSTEM

    C. Fernando, Thilina Heenatigala, Dr. K V S Prasadh, N.S.D. Withanage

    Journal of Double Star Observations, vol. 22, [2026]

    A comprehensive study of the STF 1434 double star system (also known as WDS 10274+1804 according to the Washington Double Star (WDS) catalogue) was conducted utilizing the Las Cumbres Observatory (LCO) telescope located at the Cerro Tololo observatory. The target double star was selected through a rigorous process employing Stellarium software and the Stelledoppie catalogue. Observations were carried out from December 2023 to January 2024. The data reduction phase involved utilizing AstroImageJ software, enabling precise analysis of the observed data. Additionally, historical data of the STF 1434 double star system provided by the United States Naval Observatory (USNO) was used to compare with the new measurements. Key parameters such as Position Angle (PA), Angular Separation (SEP), and Delta Magnitude were measured from the observational data. Subsequently, a comparative analysis with historical data was conducted. The results of PA 282.1737º ± 0.0655 º, SEP 6.4661"± 0.0131", and Delta Magnitude 0.0427± 0.0002 indicated that the newest measurements of PA, SEP, and Delta Magnitude were consistent with the historical data with some exceptions during some occasions.

  1. Study of Higgs Boson Mass Reconstruction in tHq, H → ττ Final States Using Machine Learning

    Chalaka Fernando (supervised within the ATLAS Collaboration)

    Technical report — CERN Summer Student Programme, [2024]

    Summer-student research project on reconstructing the Higgs boson candidate mass in tHq production with H → ττ, in final states with two light leptons and one hadronically decaying tau. Developed and evaluated neural-network regression models in TensorFlow/Keras on ATLAS analysis-level data.
